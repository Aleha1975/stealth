The problem

Your wallet is a public diary.

Share one address and anyone can see every payment you get, your balance and who pays you. Forever.

Loophole gives every payer a new address for you. On-chain they look like strangers. Only your keys tie them together.

How it works

Three steps. No setup.

01
Sign onceYour wallet signs one message. Your browser derives private keys from it.
02
Share one linkPayers open it and pay with one signature. No gas, no account.
03
Collect privatelyScan, find your payments, move them anywhere. Still no gas.
No custodyWe never hold your money or your keys. Funds go straight to addresses only you control.
No gas, everPaying and withdrawing need no ETH. A small fixed fee covers the network.
Any walletWorks with the wallet you already have. Nothing to install, nothing to sign up for.
Built on a standardERC-5564 stealth addresses. Open source, verifiable on-chain.
