How it works

The receiver publishes one static key pair. Every payer derives a new address from it; the receiver finds those addresses with a viewing key and spends with a spending key. Nothing on chain ties the addresses to each other or to the receiver.

What it does not do

It hides the receiver, not the payer: the paying wallet is public as in any transaction. And privacy ends where you end it: send everything from your stealth addresses to your known wallet and the chain links them. The app refuses the obvious mistakes and warns about the rest.
